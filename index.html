ABOUT THE CLINIC

Professional physiotherapy care in Vijayawada

Sri Venkateshwara Physiotherapy Clinic provides patient-focused physiotherapy and rehabilitation services in Bhavanipuram, Vijayawada. Treatment is tailored to the individual's condition, symptoms, movement limitations and recovery goals.

Our approach combines clinical assessment, therapeutic exercise, manual therapy and selected advanced techniques to support pain relief, mobility and functional recovery.
